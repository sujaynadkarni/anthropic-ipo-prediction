Built from the company's S-1, the estimate is nearly double what private investors paid five months ago, and almost all of it rides on one guess: how fast revenue keeps growing.

How it compares

In brief

  • Revenue is exploding. $0.4B in Q1 2025 to $11.6B in Q2 2026, growing 2.4x in the latest quarter alone.
  • Compute is the speed limit. Contracts deliver roughly $100B of compute in 2027, enough for about 1.4x quarterly growth, not 2.4x.
  • The multiple is 10–15x 2027 revenue, between NVIDIA (~8x) and Palantir (~38x), anchored on OpenAI's pending round.
  • Growth dominates. Dropping from 1.4x to 1.2x a quarter roughly halves the price.
